## About this passion project

a small free web game about running a bar that hosts themed party lines.

inspired by multi-room spots that feel more like a home for certain scenes than a regular club. the kind of place where the night has a theme, a story, and a room full of people who actually showed up for that same thing.

## why this exists

i'm an introvert. on regular parties i usually felt weird and unwelcome. i never really knew where to stand or who to talk to. the music was fine, the drinks were fine, but it still felt like i was visiting someone else's night.

the times it was different were the nights that had a real theme and a strong community of unique people. suddenly there was a reason to be there. the theme gave everyone permission to be a little more themselves, and it was easier to connect. those nights are what i want this game to be about.

## the game

you manage the bar. your main job is keeping the lines of parties going.

you decide the themes. you set the vibe for each night — the music, the concept, the little story that brings certain people out. then you watch who shows up. regulars. newcomers. Underground people and the geeks. the quiet ones who only come when the theme matches something they care about. the ones who bring a friend next time. the ones who finally feel like they belong somewhere.

it's a simple management game. no complicated systems, mostly point and click game. the fun is in the people and the nights you create for them. the game runs in the browser. it's free. it's a passion project.

Later in the development I am planing to have real life events like the corona virus or the war of the 7th in October, making choices matter!

## what you do

- plan themed parties and keep the line of nights moving
- see who comes and how the night feels for them
- notice the small things: who stays longer, who talks to who, who only appears when the theme fits
- keep the community going (or watch it drift) based on the nights you make

## status

early. still figuring out the simplest way to make this run in a browser. no accounts, no payments, just the game.

if you ever went to a night that made you feel less alone, or if you just want to talk about the idea, open an issue.
